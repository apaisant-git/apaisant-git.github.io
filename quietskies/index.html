QuietSkies

The weather, reframed for calm. QuietSkies turns the forecast into a single Calm Score: how gentle or demanding the day outside will feel, so you can plan around it.

Coming to Google Play

One calm number

A Calm Score from 0 to 100 tells you at a glance how the day will feel, with no maps to read and no numbers to decode.

Gentle heads-up

An optional quiet morning briefing, plus a soft warning when a demanding stretch, like a storm, is on the way. Never a startle.

Private and free

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Your location is used only to fetch the forecast and never stored. Free, with no catches.

QuietSkies is not an emergency or severe-weather warning service. For warnings, rely on official sources such as your local weather bureau or emergency service.
